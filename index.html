CN Delivers Strong Third Quarter Performance
CN · Oct 30 · company release · earnings
CN reported third-quarter revenue of C$4,771 million, up 15%, and adjusted diluted EPS of C$2.16, up 18%, on revenue ton-miles up 4%; grain and fertilizer carloads rose 11% and automotive revenue 31%, while coal carloads fell 11% and intermodal carloads 6%. The company still assumes low single-digit RTM growth in 2026, and separately said CFO Ghislain Houle will retire February 1, 2027, succeeded by Jamie Lockwood (per Progressive Railroading). Why it matters: strong grain and automotive gains support covered-hopper and autorack use on a major customer's network, but flat total carloads mean the revenue growth is mostly price and mix, not added car demand.

Schneider selects NS as rail partner for eastern US
Progressive Railroading · Oct 30 · trade press · intermodal · Norfolk Southern
Schneider has signed an agreement making Norfolk Southern its primary rail provider in the eastern U.S., a role CSX previously held, according to the article; no effective date was given. Schneider brings about 26,800 containers and 23,900 chassis, and NS said its intermodal volumes grew 3% in the third quarter. Why it matters: the move shifts a large eastern intermodal account between two customers rather than growing total demand, but it adds to the competitive backdrop of the UP–NS merger review.
BLET ratifies agreement on CPKC DM&E
Progressive Railroading · Oct 30 · trade press · labor · CPKC
Members of the Brotherhood of Locomotive Engineers and Trainmen ratified the five-year agreement covering about 250 train and engine employees on the southern part of CPKC's DM&E in Minnesota, Iowa, Illinois and Missouri, with wage increases and higher meal allowances. No vote tally was reported. Why it matters: ratification removes a labor uncertainty on a Midwest grain-and-ethanol corridor, a small positive for service stability there.
