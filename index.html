LAPD uncovers $1 million in stolen BNSF and Union Pacific rail cargo at a Los Angeles-area shoe retailer
FreightWaves · Oct 7 · security · police account
Police executing a search warrant on September 30 at Shoes n Shoes in Whittier, California, recovered 59 pallets of branded merchandise worth about $1 million that investigators linked to cargo stolen from BNSF and Union Pacific trains, and arrested the owner on suspicion of receiving stolen property. Rail investigators from both railroads, the Alameda Belt Line, Los Angeles Port Police and the district attorney's office took part. Why it matters: the owner told police he bought from illegitimate middlemen, and how the cargo left rail control has not been disclosed, so the scale of the underlying theft is unclear.

Regulatory & Policy 1 story
New York Gov. Hochul: tariffs pose risk to MTA fleet renewal
Progressive Railroading · Oct 7 · trade policy · state and agency claims
New York's governor, Kathy Hochul, asked Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and Trade Representative Jamieson Greer to exempt transit rolling-stock purchases from tariffs, citing an MTA analysis presented to its board in September that estimates tariffs could raise the cost of its fleet renewal by $1 billion. The MTA has committed $23 billion to new subway cars, commuter-rail cars and buses, and says more than 75% of the components in its R211 subway cars were made in the US. Why it matters: the request covers passenger equipment only, but it shows tariffs on specialized imported components raising costs even for largely domestic builds, a pressure freight car buyers may also face.
Global Rail 1 story
Max-Theurer family forms JMT Industrie Holding over Plasser & Theurer, Robel, Matisa and Seno
Progressive Railroading supplier roundup · Oct 8 · Europe · company announcement
The Austrian Max-Theurer family has placed four track construction, maintenance and rail-grinding businesses, previously held in separate structures, under a new joint holding company, with each remaining independently managed. Combined revenue is expected to be about €1.7 billion in 2026, according to the company. Why it matters: this is maintenance-of-way equipment rather than freight car components, so any effect on Amsted's markets is indirect.
