President's Brief — three takeaways
  1. Brazil has laid out a large pipeline of freight rail concessions. The federal government plans to seek private operators for four new freight corridors and four existing ones, inside a wider rail package valued at R$160 billion that also covers passenger and urban lines. Only the 575 km Southeast Railway Ring is expected to be tendered this year; for the rest, bids could be called next year. It is a plan rather than awards, so any effect on Brazilian wagon and component demand is years away and depends on the tenders going ahead.
  2. Outside North America, the weekend's wagon news was small in scale. Georgian Railway is buying 200 open wagons from a Russian repair plant for US$9.6 million. India's Jupiter Wagons signed a non-binding agreement with Poland's Pesa that is aimed mainly at locomotives and passenger trains, plus components. Neither changes the North American freight car picture.
  3. A heavy week of supplier and customer results starts today. Greenbrier reports fiscal fourth-quarter results after the market closes today, CPKC on October 28, GATX and Trinity Industries on October 29 and CN on October 30, with Timken following on November 4. New York & Atlantic's federally backed locomotive repower is the only new North American funding item this edition. The National Steel Car strike that began August 12 has not been reported settled.

Brazil announces freight, passenger and urban rail concession plans
Railway Gazette International · Oct 22 · Brazil federal government · freight concessions
Brazil's federal government plans to seek private concessionaires for four greenfield freight corridors, including the 2,400 km East-West Corridor, the 933 km Ferrogrão and the 575 km Southeast Railway Ring, which is expected to be tendered this year, and to re-award four existing corridors including Malha Oeste. The whole package, which also covers passenger and urban projects, is valued at R$160 billion, and for most projects bids could be called next year. Why it matters: if tendered and built, these lines would add to long-run wagon and component demand in Brazil, but none is yet awarded.

World rail contract news round-up: Georgian Railway open wagons
Railway Gazette International · Oct 26 · Georgian Railway · wagon order
Georgian Railway will acquire 200 open wagons from Russia's Roslavl Wagon Repair Plant under a contract worth US$9.6 million. Why it matters: it is a small order in a market outside Amsted's footprint and has no direct bearing on North American car demand.
Jupiter Wagons and Pesa to co-operate to bring Polish rail technology to India
Railway Gazette International · Oct 26 · Jupiter Wagons · India
India's Jupiter Wagons and Poland's Pesa Bydgoszcz signed a non-binding memorandum of understanding covering technology transfer and potential joint production of locomotives and passenger trains, joint decarbonisation projects including hydrogen vehicles, and component production. No values or volumes were given. Why it matters: it signals one of India's main freight wagon builders moving toward passenger and locomotive work, but it is not a freight wagon order.
