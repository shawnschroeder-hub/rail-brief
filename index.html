CSX announces retirement of Chief Operating Officer Mike Cory
CSX release via GlobeNewswire (wire mirror) · Oct 12 · leadership · company announcement
CSX said Mike Cory, executive vice president and chief operating officer since September 2023, is retiring and will work with the leadership team on the transition; the release gives no effective date. USDA raises corn yield 2.7 bpa to 181.2 bpa
DTN · Oct 9 · agriculture · USDA data
USDA's October report raised its 2026-27 U.S. corn yield estimate to 181.2 bushels per acre from 178.5 in September, lifting projected production to 16.03 billion bushels and ending stocks to 1.849 billion from 1.567 billion, as reported by DTN. Soybean production was raised to 4.562 billion bushels on a 53.1-bushel yield. Why it matters: a larger crop means more grain to move this fall, though how much reaches rail, and how fast, will depend on export demand and farmers' selling pace.
Port tracker report: Peak import season coming to a close
Progressive Railroading · Oct 9 · intermodal · NRF/Hackett Associates forecast
The NRF/Hackett Associates Global Port Tracker counted 2.3 million TEU at major U.S. container ports in August, down 0.7% from a year earlier, and forecasts gains of 8.2% in September and 8.5% in October before a 1% decline in November. It projects 25.8 million TEU for 2026, up 1.4% from 2025. Why it matters: NRF says most holiday merchandise has already arrived, so import-driven intermodal volume may ease after October; this is a signal for container traffic rather than carload equipment.

The rail freight industry concludes its first discussion of GCU overhaul
RailFreight.com · Oct 12 · Europe · industry discussion
More than 40 representatives of railway undertakings, wagon keepers and policymakers met in Bern on October 7 to discuss overhauling the General Contract of Use, the roughly 20-year-old agreement with about 840 signatories that governs how European operators use wagons owned by others. The European Rail Freight Association wants a fairer split of liability when a wagon defect occurs and clearer roles for operators, keepers and maintenance entities in pre-departure checks; the overhaul is not yet on any policy agenda. Why it matters: a shift in defect liability between operators and wagon keepers could change European maintenance and inspection practices, though the idea is at an early stage.
Germany finalises overhaul of DB's troubled corridor renovation plan, eight delayed projects named
RailFreight.com · Oct 9 · Europe · government decision
Germany has agreed a five-point overhaul of Deutsche Bahn's Generalsanierung corridor-renovation programme, keeping long line closures but committing to better freight diversions, new ETCS requirements and changes in how renovated lines reopen. Eight previously scheduled corridors, including the Bremerhaven–Bremen freight route, are missing from the revised programme. Why it matters: long closures with diversions will continue on Germany's main network, which can lengthen freight routings and wagon cycle times.
