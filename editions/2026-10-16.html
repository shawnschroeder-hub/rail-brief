Norfolk Southern Railway Derailment and Collision with Hazardous Materials Release (NTSB investigation RRD25FR019)
NTSB · finding posted by Oct 14 (Railway Age coverage Oct 15) · accident investigation · probable cause
The NTSB's probable cause for the July 18, 2025 accident near Browns, Illinois, in which 24 cars of eastbound NS train 167 derailed at a spring switch and struck a standing train, is NS's inadequate procedures to ensure proper repair, testing and track-signal coordination, which left the switch's mechanical integrity unverified and produced a gapped switch point; three hazardous-materials tank cars breached and two crew members on the standing train were injured. NS has since added a procedure requiring post-work testing on such switches, audited its system and equipped signal maintainers with the required tools. Why it matters: the stated cause is a procedural gap rather than a railcar or component defect, so it bears on railroad maintenance practice more than on equipment.
Union Pacific CFO Hamann to retire after 35 years with company
Progressive Railroading · Oct 16 · Union Pacific · executive change (UP release dated Oct 16)
Union Pacific said Executive Vice President and CFO Jennifer Hamann will retire in early 2027; she joined in 1992, has been CFO since January 2020 and sits on the boards of TTX, Grupo Ferroviario Mexicano and Steel Dynamics, and no successor was named. Hamann said she remains a strong supporter of the Norfolk Southern merger. Why it matters: the departure comes while the merger is before the Surface Transportation Board, which makes the choice of successor worth watching.
Wabtec signs R6.6bn agreement to help enhance South African freight loco performance
Railway Gazette International · Oct 16 · Wabtec · contract (announced Oct 15)
Transnet Engineering and Wabtec signed a R6.6 billion long-term Material and Technical Support Agreement to improve availability and performance of Transnet's Class 43D and 44D diesel locomotive fleets, with Wabtec supplying components and using its Expert-on-Alert condition-monitoring platform for predictive maintenance; the article gives no contract term. Why it matters: it is a locomotive contract outside North American freight cars, but it shows Wabtec pairing parts supply with digital condition monitoring in a single long-term service deal.

Railway supply industry news round-up (Bulmarket Group, Ruse workshop)
Railway Gazette International · Oct 16 · Bulgaria · wagon manufacturing and maintenance
Bulgarian private freight operator Bulmarket Group has spent €60 million restoring the former locomotive and wagon workshop at Ruse, which closed in 2013 and was bought by the company in December 2020, to serve as a centre for wagon manufacturing and rolling stock maintenance, with about 600 jobs targeted eventually. Why it matters: it adds privately funded wagon-building and maintenance capacity in southeastern Europe, though the round-up gives no output or customer detail.
