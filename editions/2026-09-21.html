President's Brief — three takeaways
  1. The Surface Transportation Board kept the UP–Norfolk Southern merger alive, denying motions from BNSF, CSX and a shipper coalition to dismiss the revised application outright. The case now runs on a schedule with opening comments due November 18, 2026 and responses due February 16, 2027; the Board stressed the ruling "does not reflect any determination on the merits." Union Pacific separately reported more than 500 customers have now formally backed the combination. The structural question for the industry — one combined transcontinental railroad versus today's four-carrier landscape — stays open well into 2027.
  2. The FRA finalized a rule, effective September 30, expanding the permitted wheel-diameter variation for AC-traction locomotives. It responds to a 2019 Class I petition and advances in traction-motor control, and it applies to locomotive wheel sets, not freight-car wheels, so it does not change the standards Amsted Rail's freight-car wheel business operates under. It is nonetheless a reminder that FRA is actively revisiting wheel-set tolerances, worth watching for any freight-car-side follow-on.
  3. The freight-car fleet held in storage fell to 18.1% in August, down from 21.7% in January, with more than 59,000 cars pulled out of storage since the start of the year. That is happening even as AAR's latest weekly count showed total carloads and intermodal volume down 3.7% year over year for the week ending September 12, though year-to-date volume is still up 2.7% for carloads and 4.0% for intermodal through 36 weeks. Fewer idle cars alongside a soft single week but a positive year-to-date trend is worth tracking as a demand signal for new-build and aftermarket component orders.

STB denies motions seeking summary denial of the UP–NS merger
Surface Transportation Board · Sep 18 · regulatory · UP–NS merger · standing watch item
The Surface Transportation Board denied motions from BNSF, CSX and a shipper coalition seeking summary dismissal of the revised Union Pacific–Norfolk Southern merger application, allowing the case to proceed on a schedule with opening comments due November 18, 2026 and responses due February 16, 2027. The Board said the ruling "does not reflect any determination on the merits of the proposed transaction," and Union Pacific separately reported more than 500 customers have formally backed the combination. Why it matters: the merger stays alive and on a multi-year track, keeping the industry's biggest structural question open well into 2027.
FRA finalizes wider wheel-diameter tolerance for AC-traction locomotives
Federal Register / FRA · effective Sep 30 · regulatory · wheels
The FRA finalized a rule, effective September 30, 2026, expanding the permitted diameter variation for AC-traction locomotive wheel sets, responding to a Class I railroad's 2019 petition and advances in traction-motor control technology. The change applies to locomotive wheel sets, not freight-car wheel standards. Why it matters: it is a direct, if narrow, product-standard change in the wheel space Amsted Rail operates in, even though it applies to locomotives rather than freight cars.
Freight-car storage keeps falling even as weekly traffic dips
Trade-press reporting on fleet data · Aug 2026 (monthly figure) · forecasts & market intelligence
The freight-car fleet held in storage fell to 18.1% in August, down from 21.7% in January, with more than 59,000 cars pulled out of storage since the start of the year. Why it matters: fewer idle cars is a demand signal worth tracking for new-build and aftermarket component orders, even as weekly carload volume has been choppy.

AAR: weekly rail traffic down 3.7% but year-to-date volume still positive
Association of American Railroads · week ending Sep 12 · associations
AAR's weekly rail traffic report for the week ending September 12 showed total U.S. carloads and intermodal volume of 494,865 units, down 3.7% from the same week in 2025 — carloads fell 3.3% to 223,560 and intermodal fell 4.1% to 271,305. Grain (+3,461 carloads), petroleum products (+663) and forest products (+174) rose from a year earlier, while motor vehicles and parts (-3,313), chemicals (-3,195) and coal (-2,229) fell; year-to-date carloads are still up 2.7% and intermodal up 4.0% through 36 weeks. Why it matters: a soft single week sits inside an up year-to-date trend, consistent with the improving storage picture above rather than contradicting it.
Regulatory & Policy 1 story
FRA issues Safety Advisory on restricted-speed compliance
Federal Register / FRA · Sep 3 · regulatory · safety
The FRA issued Safety Advisory 2026-01, reminding railroads and employees to comply with restricted-speed rules after reviewing seven recent train accidents where crews failed to operate at restricted speed, with particular emphasis on positive train control's Restricted Mode. The advisory is non-binding guidance, not a new rule. Why it matters: it signals FRA attention on restricted-speed compliance that could inform future enforcement or rulemaking.
