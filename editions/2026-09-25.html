Edition 029 · Friday, September 25, 2026 · 9 stories
President's Brief — three takeaways
  1. US rail traffic rebounded in the week ending September 19, up 4.9% year over year, per AAR data. Intermodal rose 6.9% and carloads 2.4%, with seven of ten carload groups up, led by metallic ores and metals (+15.1%) and petroleum products (+12.5%), while coal (-3.1%) and grain (-7.7%) fell. Year-to-date US volume is up 3.4%, and Mexican carloads rose 16.9% for the week. One week is noisy — it follows a down week — but a positive year-to-date trend is generally supportive of running-repair wear-part demand, without mapping directly onto new-car orders.
  2. Competitors are locking in long-term fleet relationships abroad, especially in Saudi Arabia and Brazil. Wabtec signed a roughly US$254 million deal with Brazil's MRS Logística covering Evolution Series locomotives, spare parts and maintenance, phased through 2032, and Progress Rail won an order for 33 EMD heavy-haul locomotives from Saudi Arabia Railways, plus a localization memorandum. These follow Greenbrier's 780-car SAR order reported earlier this week. These are locomotive and service deals, not freight-car components, but they show rivals bundling equipment with lifecycle service and local-content commitments in markets where Amsted has or seeks a footprint.
  3. Two FRA final rules take effect next Wednesday, September 30, and third-quarter earnings season is now scheduled. The brake system rule codifies long-running locomotive brake waivers and amends brake inspection standards for passenger and freight equipment; a separate rule repeals obsolete roadway worker safety requirements. Union Pacific will report on October 22, the day after CSX on October 21. Railroad guidance on volumes, fleet size and capital spending in those calls will be the next read on demand heading into 2027.

US freight rail traffic rises 4.9% in week ending September 19
Progressive Railroading, citing AAR data · Sep 24 · data · traffic
US railroads moved 535,663 carloads and intermodal units (+4.9%), including 234,207 carloads (+2.4%) and 301,456 intermodal units (+6.9%), with gains in seven of ten carload groups; coal fell 3.1%, grain 7.7% and motor vehicles and parts 0.8%. Year-to-date through 36 weeks, US volume is up 3.4%, Canada 1.4% and Mexico 7.4%. Why it matters: the rebound follows the prior week's 3.7% dip and keeps the year-to-date trend positive, though a single week is not a trend on its own.
Wabtec signs US$254 million locomotive, parts and maintenance deal with Brazil's MRS Logística
Railway Supply, citing Wabtec and MRS · announced Sep 23, published Sep 25 · competitors · global
The R$1.3 billion (about US$254 million) agreement covers Evolution Series locomotives including the ES44ACi, plus spare parts and specialist maintenance, phased through 2032; neither company disclosed the locomotive count or the split between equipment and services. It follows MRS's February 2024 purchase of 30 ES44ACi locomotives. Why it matters: Wabtec is deepening a bundled equipment-plus-service relationship with a major Brazilian heavy-haul railroad, a market where Amsted Maxion operates.
FRA brake system and roadway worker final rules take effect September 30
Federal Register (FRA final rules, published Aug 31; effective date per today's Register listing) · effective Sep 30 · regulatory · safety
The brake system final rule amends brake inspection standards for passenger and freight equipment and writes long-standing locomotive brake maintenance waivers into the regulations; a companion final rule repeals obsolete roadway worker safety requirements and creates a special-approval path for alternative bridge-worker safety approaches. Both take effect Wednesday, September 30. Why it matters: because the brake rule largely formalizes relief already operating by waiver, the near-term effect on brake component demand should be limited.

Saudi Arabia Railways orders 33 EMD locomotives from Progress Rail and plans localization
Railway Gazette International · Sep 25 · competitors · orders
Announced at InnoTrans, the order for 33 SD70ACS heavy-haul diesel locomotives takes SAR's EMD fleet above 150 units, with a memorandum of understanding to explore localization, workforce development and supply-chain opportunities. SAR says the units will support phosphate traffic on its North Railway and growing mining and industrial demand. Why it matters: combined with Greenbrier's 780-car SAR order this week, it points to active Saudi freight fleet expansion — and local-content expectations that could shape future component sourcing there.
Greenbrier Europe to produce and lease NIK cradles for putting truck trailers on rail
Railway Gazette International · Sep 24 · competitors · intermodal
TX Logistik and Greenbrier Europe signed an initial order for 50 NIK lifting cradles at InnoTrans on September 23, letting non-craneable semi-trailers be lifted onto standard pocket wagons without changes to terminals or wagons. Greenbrier Europe will handle production and leasing, and TX Logistik commercial development. Why it matters: a small order, but it shows Greenbrier building leasing and intermodal niches in Europe beyond conventional wagon building.

Union Pacific takes delivery of first Wabtec battery-electric locomotives
Progressive Railroading, citing Union Pacific · Sep 24 · customers · locomotives
UP received two Wabtec FLXdrive battery-electric locomotives, each with about 7,000 battery cells and 2.7 megawatt-hours of storage, for testing in switching and local service in the Los Angeles area, with two more due next month. Why it matters: this is a four-unit yard and local-service test, not a fleet decision, and it does not change freight-car component demand.

STB appoints Director of Office of Economics and Senior Advisor to the Chairman
Surface Transportation Board (press release) · Sep 24 · regulatory · personnel
Marty Schlenker, formerly with Parallel Systems, CSX and BNSF, becomes Director of the Office of Economics, overseeing the Board's data gathering and reporting, and Terrence McDermott, a former Bunge supply-chain director with Ferromex experience, becomes Senior Advisor to the Chairman. Both previously held temporary transition roles at the Board. Why it matters: the economics office supports the analytical work behind major cases, including the UP–NS merger review now under way.

Railway Gazette International · Sep 25 · global · Europe
European locomotive lessor Railpool became launch customer for the Vectron X with 30 electric and 50 dual-mode units firm plus an option for 20 more, first deliveries in 2027, value undisclosed; RDC Deutschland separately ordered two dual-mode units for about €10 million.
