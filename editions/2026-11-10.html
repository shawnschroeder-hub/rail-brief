Morristown & Erie Railway enters partnership with Railroad Development Corp.
Progressive Railroading · Nov 9 · trade press · short line · ownership
In a restructuring effective October 31, individuals affiliated with Railroad Development Corp. took 50% of the New Jersey short line Morristown & Erie, with legacy shareholders keeping the other half. The railroad owns 10.5 miles of track, holds trackage rights over 85 miles of NJ Transit lines, interchanges with CSX and Norfolk Southern, and runs a locomotive and car repair shop in Morristown. Why it matters: it is a small deal, but it adds an experienced operator to an Eastern short line that also does car repair work.

Rail Vision Successfully Completes U.S. Rail Industry Testing Phase Under the AAR's Technology Roadmap Program
Rail Vision release (read via a StockTitan mirror) · Nov 10 · company release · AAR / MxV Rail program · automation
Rail Vision said its TrackScout mainline system completed a testing phase in the AAR Technology Roadmap's train control, communications and operations program, managed by MxV Rail and aimed at restricted-speed enforcement. The company disclosed no test results or commercial agreement. Why it matters: it shows the AAR program moving AI perception technology toward industry standards, though this is a vendor's account of one step.
Global Rail 1 story
Jupiter Wagons Secures Orders Worth Rs 324 Crore From GATX India, Chetak International
Autocar Professional, citing Jupiter Wagons · Nov 2 · trade press · India · automobile-carrier wagons
Jupiter Wagons received a letter of intent from GATX India for 10 rakes, or 270 ACT2 automobile-carrier wagons, valued at Rs 214.94 crore, plus a firm order from Chetak International for 135 wagons worth Rs 109.10 crore. Why it matters: GATX's Indian unit is building a private auto-rack fleet, though the GATX document is a letter of intent and its value may change.
